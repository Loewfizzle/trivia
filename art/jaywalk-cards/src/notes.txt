     asked to push the voxel look and did not really move it.

     important at the edges”).

     corner only). The saucer also still trails pink/cyan off its right lip,
     which was not requested.
  5. CLOSE ENCOUNTER take 1 (the backup) missed the cars entirely.
  6. RIGHT OF WAY take 1 (the recommended use file) has very small lettering
     on the snapback cap strap, readable in a tight zoom as something like
     “WIKI & WE”. It is not readable at phone-card size, but it is text, and
     the brief forbids text. Background neon stays abstract colour blocks;
     a couple of signs are letter-like but not clearly readable words.
  7. RIGHT OF WAY take 2 (backup) removed the cap lettering but also lost
     the sneakers (dark block shoes instead of the pink/white pair).
  8. Voxel city neon in all three is blocky and unused as copy, but a few
     signs are vaguely glyph-like (not readable logos or words).
