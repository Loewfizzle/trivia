NEON SPEAKEASY - JUGGLER: CLAUDE NOTES (2026-11-01)
Scene: a juggler drops a ball in a lit window across the street. It thuds on a wooden floor, bounces a couple of times
and rolls to a stop. It should be heard through glass: quiet and muffled, under music, mostly on phone speakers.
How I checked: I could not listen, so I used spectrograms, a 2-5 ms level trace (take and bounce times), EBU R128 loudness
(LUFS integrated over the whole file, including silence), true peak, and clipping tests on the originals (full-scale
samples, float overs, flat-top runs). Every delivered file passed. Files are untouched originals with original names.
The builder trims and converts. Times are seconds from the start of the file. "rel" = dB below that file's loudest hit.

== MIXING THE SCENE ==
- Muffle for "through a window across the street": low-pass at about 1-2 kHz (start at 1.5 kHz, 12-24 dB/oct). A
  gentle high-pass at about 80-100 Hz helps phone speakers and keeps the thud from booming.
- Add a small-room reverb (short, about 0.3-0.5 s decay, low wet) so it sits inside the bar, not dry in the street.
- Play it at a very low level under the music: about -30 to -36 LUFS short-term is a starting point. The thud should be felt
  more than heard. Combine one drop take with one thud if a single file sounds thin.
- Trims, fades (2-5 ms in, 50-150 ms out), level, mono fold-down and conversion to game format are done by the builder,
  not on these originals.
