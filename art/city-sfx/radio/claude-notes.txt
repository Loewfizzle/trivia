NEON SPEAKEASY - RADIO DESK: CLAUDE NOTES (2026-11-02)
Scene: Eddie's private city control panel on his phone, styled as a 1920s walnut-and-brass radio desk.
Desk sounds (jack, fire, knob, band button, denied, cover) are for Eddie only, on a phone speaker: crisp, mechanical, premium
(brass and Bakelite), never plasticky, cartoony or loud. The PA chime and PA crackle are heard by every visitor on phone speakers
over music, so they must be clear and classy.
How I checked: I could not listen, so I used a 2-5 ms level trace (take times), spectral centroid and low-frequency share,
pitch tracking (chime notes), EBU R128 loudness (LUFS integrated over the whole file, including silence), true peak, and clipping
tests on the originals (full-scale samples, float overs, flat-top runs). Every delivered file passed unless noted.
Files are untouched originals with original names. The builder trims, converts and normalises.
Times are seconds from the start of the file. "rel" = dB below that file's loudest point. "Gated" = digital silence between sounds.
Very short one-shots fall below the LUFS gate; judge them by peak.
Main folder = picks; alternates/ = weaker but usable extras.

== GAPS AND HONEST CAVEATS ==
- jack-in/out: no real brass switchboard jack exists in any free CC0/Sonniss/Mixkit source I could find. All jacks are modern
  3.5/6.35 mm plugs. The brassiest option is to layer a jack seat with a short metal click (e.g. Latchlocker in alternates/).
- pa-chime: none is a live recording of a vibraphone or tubular bell. 419491 is a sampled vibraphone; 244583 is a clean
  recreated airplane cabin chime (pure sine). If none sounds classy enough, synthesize a two-tone vibraphone ding-dong
  (e.g. E5 -> C5, soft mallet, 1.8 s decay).
== AIRPLANE CABIN CHIME RANKING (all CC0, Freesound) ==
 1. 670297 Kinoton "Airplane, Seatbelt Sign Beep" (main folder) - classic D5 -> B4 two-tone, cleanest and most authentic. THE AIRPLANE PICK.
 2. 845147 Sadiquecat "Airplane ding" (main folder) - same D5 -> B4, tighter (1.7 s), louder, shorter decays.
 3. 244583 nachosose "A320 tritone chime" (main folder) - three tones G4 - D5 - B4 (crew-announcement style).
 4. 245851 Shamrock132 "chime.wav" (alternates) - real A320 recording; cabin hum at about -35 dB and handset clicks.
 Compared with the others: airplane chimes are pure sine, so the clearest on phones over music, but plainer than Mixkit 109
 (warm doorbell with overtones) or 419491 (sampled vibraphone). Mixkit 1569 is a sine-like airport three-tone in between.
 If Eddie wants the "airplane" feel: use 670297 trimmed 0.27-2.40, normalised.
