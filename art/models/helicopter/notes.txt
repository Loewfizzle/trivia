NEON SPEAKEASY - PATROL / PARTY HELICOPTER - NOTES (2026-10-30)

BEST PICK: helicopter-utility-hd_3dassets-18712.glb
  What it is : a generic light single-turbine civil helicopter in the Bell 206 JetRanger style (bubble nose,
               engine-deck fairing, thin tail boom, T-tail with an orange fin, tubular skids). Navy-blue paint,
               ivory boom and roof, orange accent fin, plus a small "light" material (a lamp) on the boom.
               It reads as police / news / tour. No floats, winch, weapons or other attachments.
  Format     : glTF 2.0 binary (.glb), single self-contained file, no textures (7 flat PBR materials),
               uses KHR_mesh_quantization (three.js GLTFLoader loads it natively, with no decoder needed).
  Size       : 318,796 bytes (311 KB)
  Triangles  : 6,456 (11 meshes, 14 nodes). The rotors are 1,424 of these.
  Units/axes : metres, +Y up, nose toward +Z, origin at the footprint centre on the ground (y=0).
               Overall 8.50 (rotor span, X) x 3.115 (height) x 9.56 (length incl. rotor disc) m.
  Animation  : one clip "rotor-spin" (LINEAR, 0-1 s, rotation channels on main-rotor and tail-rotor only).
               The asset page text says "no animation clips", but the file does contain this one. Ignore it if unused.

  NODE HIERARCHY (confirmed with gltf-transform + three.js; rotors ARE separate):
    utility-helicopter                  (root group)
      utility-helicopter_0   mat paint   (fuselage shell)           1,856 tris
      utility-helicopter_1   mat glass   (canopy glazing)             944
      utility-helicopter_2   mat ivory   (roof/boom/upper body)       976
      utility-helicopter_3   mat metal   (skids, struts, mast)        560
      utility-helicopter_4   mat accent  (orange tail fin)            176
      utility-helicopter_5   mat rubber  (dark trim/engine deck)      360
      utility-helicopter_6   mat light   (small lamp on the boom)     160
      main-rotor             GROUP, pivot (0, 3.01, 0.53). Spins about +Y.
        main-rotor/rubber    4 blades, span +/-4.25 m                 512
        main-rotor/metal     hub                                       48
      tail-rotor             GROUP, pivot (0.18, 2.20, -4.13). Spins about X (disc lies in the YZ plane, on the +X/right side of the fin)
        tail-rotor/rubber    blades, dia ~1.3 m                       768
        tail-rotor/metal     hub                                       96
    To hide the rotors: set visible=false on the nodes named "main-rotor" and "tail-rotor" (each one hides its whole group).
    The mast stub stays on the body (in utility-helicopter_3), which works well as the anchor for a code-drawn rotor disc.
    Rotor pivots are exactly at the hub centres: the blade bounding-box centres match the node origins. A disc drawn
    at world (0, 3.01, 0.53) with a radius of about 4.25 m matches the original. The tail disc is centred at (0.18, 2.20, -4.13) with a radius of about 0.65 m.

  WHY IT WON: CC0, a direct download with no account, a clean single GLB, a civil JetRanger shape, rotors as properly
    pivoted named groups, a good triangle count for sprite baking, and the cleanest silhouette of everything tested. The
    silhouette strip (previews/best_silhouette-strip_64px_8yaw.png: top row with rotors, bottom row with them hidden, at 64 px)
    reads clearly as a helicopter at every 45 degrees.

  HONEST FLAWS:
    - It is AI-generated (the site says so) and a generic design, not an exact Bell model. Details are simple, but the shape is clean.
    - The navy paint is dark. On a night scene it may sink into the background. Recolour the "paint" material
      (e.g. white/blue police livery) or add a rim light when baking. Glass is near-black.
    - The main rotor has 4 blades (a real 206 has 2). This doesn't matter if the rotors are hidden and drawn in code.
    - Fairly glossy materials (metalness 0.3-0.85), so it can look dark under flat lighting without an environment map.
    - Front and rear views are very narrow (a thin shape at 64 px), which is normal for a helicopter.

RUNNER-UP INCLUDED: runnerup_helicopter-utility-lowpoly_3dassets-20028.glb  (CC0, 122,100 bytes, 2,408 tris)
  Same author/style, simpler low-poly JetRanger shape: teal body, cream boom, orange engine fairing/fin. No textures, no animation.
  Rotors separate, but as 4 flat sibling nodes (no pivot groups): two nodes both named "main-rotor" (blades and hub, node
  origin at about (0, 2.85-2.87, 0.69)) and two nodes both named "tail-rotor" (origin at about (0.17-0.20, 2.16, -3.59)). Hiding by name works.
  It's a good fallback if the HD one is too detailed or too dark. It wasn't picked because it's less refined and the rotor nodes aren't grouped.
