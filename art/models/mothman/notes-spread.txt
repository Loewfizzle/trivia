THREE.JS
- Load with GLTFLoader plus loader.setMeshoptDecoder(MeshoptDecoder) from 'three/addons/libs/meshopt_decoder.module.js'.
  Without the decoder the small copy will not load (EXT_meshopt_compression is required). WebP textures (EXT_texture_webp)
  work in all current phone browsers. The original needs no decoder.
- The material has KHR_materials_specular with specularColorFactor 2.0 (same as the tucked model). Under bright
  room/environment lighting the very dark texture (average RGB ~32/255) therefore reads as mid-to-light grey and shiny.
  In a dark neon city scene it will look much darker. Lower the env intensity or specular if it looks too pale.
