HOW THEY WERE MADE
  Grok Imagine cannot make 2048×1536 natively (max wide size is 1280×720),
  so nothing was stretched up from a single sheet render. Six 1280×720
  images were generated, each with TWO large, widely spaced sugar-maple
  leaves on a flat chroma-green background. Leaves were chroma-keyed
  (not rembg), despilled so G never exceeds R, hardened to a bold matte,
  and placed on an exact #00FF00 canvas.

  Grid: 4 columns × 3 rows, cells 512×512 (2048/4 and 1536/3). Each leaf

FLAGS / CANDID MISSES
  1. The leaves are more photographic than “painterly-realistic”.
     Veins, curl and texture are there, but they read as photos of
     maple leaves, not painted ones.
  2. “A few turned / slightly curled”: several leaves are rotated or
     lightly angled (06, 07, 08, 11, 12). None is a strong three-quarter
     curl; the turn is modest.
  3. gen1’s source background was a darker green (about 1,139,5), not
     #00FF00. It keyed cleanly; a few leftover olive edge pixels were
     despilled (G clamped to R) before compositing.
  4. A few stems are a slightly deeper gold or red than the blade
     (especially 09 and 10). They are warm, not brown/dead, and not green.
  5. gen6-L (11) has a more mottled, slightly dry-looking surface. It is
     still amber-gold, not a brown dead leaf.
  6. No backup files. Two of eight generations were left unused.
