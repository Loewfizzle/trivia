# FREEFALL — the pictures

- `walls/*-night.webp` · `walls/*-day.webp` — the walls of the shaft by night + by day: created with Grok Imagine for this game
  (the owner's delivery, `Downloads/grok/freefall/backdrops/` — its `notes.txt`).
- `title/lettering-*.webp` · `title/tagline-*.webp` — THE OPENING's title (title-05): lettering made with Grok for this game —
  no third-party credit needed (`Downloads/grok/freefall/final/notes.txt`).
- `fx/blaze.webp` — THE BLAZE's flame sprites: `flame_01`, `flame_03`, `fire_01`, `smoke_01` from **Particle Pack** by Kenney Vleugels (kenney.nl),
  Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0 — http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/); four of its sprites, greyscale, in one atlas.
- `sky/rooftop-night.webp` · `sky/rooftop-day.webp` — THE ROOFTOP's 360° panoramas: made with Grok for this game (approved —
  no third-party credit needed; `Downloads/grok/freefall/rooftop/`).
- `roof/below-night.webp` · `roof/below-day.webp` · `roof/city-night.webp` · `roof/city-day.webp` — THE ROOF BELOW (the building's own
  roof seen from above) + the city round it (the same paintings' borders): made with Grok for this game — no third-party credit
  needed (`Downloads/grok/freefall/rooftop/notes-roof-below.txt`).
- `fx/burst.webp` — THE BREAKTHROUGHS' sprites: `smoke_07`, `smoke_04`, `circle_05`, `circle_02`, `dirt_02`, `star_06`, `scorch_01` from the same **Particle Pack** by Kenney Vleugels
  (kenney.nl), CC0 1.0; seven of its sprites, greyscale, in one atlas.
- `fx/sand.webp` · `fx/sand-normal.webp` — THE BOTTOM's seabed: **Ground 055 S** (colour + normal) by ambientCG (Lennart Demes), CC0 1.0 —
  https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Ground055S ; 1024 px.

`python -P -E scripts/build-freefall-assets.py` builds them; the delivery is only read.
