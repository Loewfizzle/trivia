# FREEFALL — the pictures

- `walls/*-night.webp` · `walls/*-day.webp` — the walls of the shaft by night + by day: created with Grok Imagine for this game
  (the owner's delivery, `Downloads/grok/freefall/backdrops/` — its `notes.txt`).
- `title/lettering-*.webp` · `title/tagline-*.webp` — THE OPENING's title (title-05): lettering made with Grok for this game —
  no third-party credit needed (`Downloads/grok/freefall/final/notes.txt`).
- `fx/blaze.webp` — THE BLAZE's flame sprites: `flame_01`, `flame_03`, `fire_01`, `smoke_01` from **Particle Pack** by Kenney Vleugels (kenney.nl),
  Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0 — http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/); four of its sprites, greyscale, in one atlas.
- THE ROOFTOP's skyline is JAYWALK's painting (`assets/jaywalk/skyline-night.webp` · `skyline-day.webp`).

`python -P -E scripts/build-freefall-assets.py` builds them; the delivery is only read.
