### Group 0 — the two reds
- **LOAD WEATHER (red on every harbor run since 9e)** — the cause, from the cloud logs: when `/api/sky` answers AFTER the reveal (the
  6 s case) the overcast darkens the sky → `DK` → the windows' share / bias / night step → the LIT windows re-baked with a 1.2 s
  crossfade (`lit crossfade · share, bias, night`). The bakes, the sky + the clouds already rode the weather's slow blend (`WX_BLEND`,
  6 s); the lit layer didn't. Now `rebakeLights()` (scene.js) crossfades over `WX_BLEND` when the weather just changed (`wxRecent()`)
  and only share / bias / warm / night moved — logged `(the weather: slow)`, not a visible row. It failed by day only (the overcast moves
  the daylight darkness most), which is when the cloud ran.
  Two more late re-bakes found on the way (the JUST ME holiday-night check, SP4's known issue): **the party windows** were keyed on
  `PT.on`, which waits for the title's entrance light + the sign's catch (it lags a panel's clock jump by ~1 s) → the windows' party look
  now reads the city's own darkness (`PT.litOn`); **the roof billboard's game** was synced only once the workers drew (after the reveal)
  → `boardSync()` runs before the reveal too. The `load` group: 15/15 locally (was 13/15).
- **THE TR-3B's TAPE STOP LAGGING** — measured (Chrome, DPR 3, 4× CPU, the owner's link, a sampler every 40 ms): the lights' first dip
  at **2.67 s**, the tape audibly bent (< 0.98 ×) only at **4.71 s** (~2 s late), 0.6 × at 8.9 s vs the lights all dark at 8.8. Not 9d's
  `held` (it needs `&lgs=`), not the crawl step: (1) the tape's sag started 1 s after the lights' (`smooth(3.0, 9.5)` vs the blackout's
  `smooth(2.0, 9.5)`), (2) the first bend waited for the rate to fall 2 %, then each 1.2 s ramp aimed at the envelope's value NOW (always
  behind), (3) a run forced at the load got ONE try at preparing its slice (s < 2.5) — before the first tap had started the music, so the
  slice was fetched + decoded only when the first bend asked (seconds on an iPhone). Now: the sag IS the blackout's own curve
  (core/harbor-legend.js#tr3bAt), the tape is ENGAGED at an inaudible 0.998 × just before the dip (its 0.35 s crossfade done before the
  sag), each ramp aims `BEND_AHEAD` (0.6 s) ahead on the envelope at every 1 % step, the prepare retries until the song plays.
  **After: the lights 2.63–2.67 s, the tape under 0.98 × at 2.81–2.88 s; 0.52 × as the city goes dark (8.75 s)** — e2e `tape`
  (`9f — THE TAPE ON THE DRAIN` + `ON TIME` + the drain's frames).
- **The stutter**: the drain's frames profiled (s 2.8 → 7 vs a calm 12 → 16): the crisp layer's `begin()` read `canvas.clientWidth`
  EVERY FRAME (a forced layout — the TR-3B's sign glitch restyles the page every frame, so each read paid a whole page layout: 138 ms vs

  lights themselves (the per-light stutter); its longest frame on the e2e page 100 ms.

### Decisions made
- LOAD WEATHER: the CAUSE fixed (the lit layer rides the weather blend), the check unchanged (a lit crossfade after the reveal IS a
  change the eye sees; the weather's own slow blend is the design and is logged as such).
- The tape: tied to the lights' own curve rather than re-tuned by hand — it can't drift from the drain again.
- The joggers: a lane, not a weight — "one every 1–2 min" is a schedule, a weight can't promise it.
- The implosion's old building is baked from the layout (skyBlock), not lifted from the city's bake canvas — a page opened mid-implosion
  (or a panel preview) has no "before" bake to lift from. The neighbours' cast shadows on it are left out (by day, 10 am, small).
- The sounds: "keep tower-horn.wav / hammering.wav / crane.wav" read as the slots' CURRENT takes (warning-horn, hammering-far,
  crane-work — the files 9e imported); the folder's same-named WAVs (Grok's first Oct 28 set) were not imported.
